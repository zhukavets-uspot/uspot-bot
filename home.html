What is Uspot?

Uspot is a beauty booking platform that connects clients with beauty masters (hairdressers, nail technicians, makeup artists, and more) in Minsk, Belarus.

Clients discover masters, view their portfolios and reviews, and book appointments — all inside Telegram via our Mini App and bot.

Open in Telegram

How it works

  • Browse beauty masters and their services.
  • Pick a date and time that suits you.
  • Receive instant Telegram notifications for confirmations, reminders, and updates.
  • Leave a review after your appointment.

Beauty masters manage bookings through a dedicated dashboard and optionally sync confirmed appointments to their Google Calendar automatically.
