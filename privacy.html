Uspot Privacy Policy

Beauty booking service — Minsk, Belarus

Last updated: May 10, 2026

1. Who We Are

Uspot ("we", "our", "the Service") is a beauty booking platform that connects clients with beauty masters in Minsk, Belarus. The Service operates through a Telegram Mini App and a companion Telegram bot.

Contact: 2zhukavets@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the booking service:

  • Telegram account data — your Telegram user ID and first name, received when you open the Mini App or start the bot. We do not collect your phone number or Telegram username.
  • Booking information — the service you booked, the master, date, time, and price.
  • Optional client notes — any notes or photo you attach to a booking request.
  • Reviews and ratings — text and photos you submit after a completed session.
  • Portfolio uploads — photos uploaded by beauty masters to their profiles.
  • Feedback messages — messages sent via the in-app feedback form.
  • Google Calendar tokens — OAuth tokens for beauty masters who voluntarily connect their Google Calendar (see Section 5).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Send you Telegram notifications about your bookings (confirmation, reminders 24 h and 1 h before, status updates).
  • Allow beauty masters to manage their schedule and confirm or decline bookings.
  • Display your reviews and ratings on master profile pages (after moderation approval).
  • Respond to feedback messages you send to our team.
  • Generate anonymised analytics for internal business reporting (booking counts, revenue).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

All booking and user data is stored in Supabase (supabase.com), a cloud database hosted in the EU region. Supabase applies encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).

The Telegram bot and API server run on Railway (railway.app), a cloud platform with encrypted connections.

We retain your data for as long as your bookings exist in the system. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us at 2zhukavets@gmail.com.

5. Google Calendar Integration

Beauty masters (not clients) may optionally connect their Google account to automatically sync confirmed bookings into a dedicated "Uspot Bookings" calendar. This integration is entirely voluntary.

When a master connects Google Calendar, we request the following permission:

  • calendar.events — create, update, and delete events in the master's Google Calendar. We use this scope exclusively to:
    • Create a calendar event when a booking is confirmed.
    • Delete the calendar event if the booking is cancelled.

We do not read the content of any existing calendar events. We do not access any other Google services. We do not share Google Calendar data with any third party.

OAuth tokens are stored securely in our database and used only to perform the actions described above. Masters can revoke access at any time via Google Account → Third-party apps or by contacting us.

Our use of Google Calendar API data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data.
  • Withdrawal of consent — revoke Google Calendar access or opt out of Telegram notifications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at 2zhukavets@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Tracking

The Uspot Mini App and moderation dashboard do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. The only external requests are to Supabase (database), Google Fonts (typography), and the Telegram WebApp API — all strictly necessary for the Service to function.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
